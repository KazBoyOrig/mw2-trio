# Changelog

## 0.0.1-dev — 2026-10-21

Development source snapshot, pre-alpha.

- x86 system-winmm proxy and strict build184 fingerprint guard.
- Opt-in main-thread control and verified pre-WinMain component installation.
- Guarded three-record player storage and a three-record cache reserve with native traversal kept at two.
- Exact-build GSC source loader, pinned MIT framework content and own player-count probe.
- Own helper suite and an offline roster/readiness candidate with mutation tests.
- English build, test, architecture and roadmap documentation; preserved upstream credits and licenses.
- Guarded native two-player UDP admission, socket/connect adapters and campaign runtime support.
- Exact-peer development endpoint configuration, owned-adapter/socket checks and role-distinct cached identities.
- Automated same-PC campaign trainer movement checks: seven phases passed over loopback and exact-peer transport, with matching measured world positions and normal exits.
- Opt-in developer host/join launcher and public read-only QA readers with synthetic regression fixtures.

The two-player evidence covers two processes on one PC and the trainer's pit start. Separate-PC connectivity, aim/fire/use, death/revive, checkpoints and mission progression remain unverified. Three/four-player networking is not implemented. There is no complete two-player campaign or playable three-player release.
